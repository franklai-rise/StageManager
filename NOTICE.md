## Lai v1 modification scope

The fork adds fixed leftmost-monitor placement, multi-window visibility, Windows shell and Yuanbao
exclusions, desktop-icon preservation, hook cleanup, startup-race protection, compact sidebar cards,
and automatic card scaling for large application lists.
